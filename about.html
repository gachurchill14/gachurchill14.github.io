About

Medical writing informed by science, shaped by clarity.

Hello, I'm Geoff Churchill.

I'm a freelance medical writer and science communicator based in France, working with charities, healthcare organisations and life science companies to create accurate, engaging content that makes complex science accessible.

My background is unusual for a medical writer. Before moving into healthcare communications I spent over a decade working as a biologist, conservation scientist and contract manager in New Zealand. That experience taught me how to analyse evidence, communicate with diverse audiences and manage complex projects from start to finish.

Today I combine those scientific skills with specialist training in medical writing to produce clear, trustworthy content for patients, healthcare professionals and the public.
